## 1. Destination (working, not yet canonical)

**Effective AI Engineering Partnership:** Anhar can bring a concrete engineering task or an ambiguous problem to AI Agent(s), obtain useful challenge and feasibility assessment, identify a fit-for-purpose solution, use suitable specialized expertise/tools, and reach trustworthy verified engineering outcomes with clear visibility and proportionate Human intervention.

Quality, authority, safety, and reconstructable state remain important; orchestration/automation, fixed stages, Space, and parallel execution are not goals in themselves.

## 1A. Station 1 — Rediscover Purpose (Anhar-approved retrospective baseline)

**Progress:** COMPLETE — Anhar approved Station 1 on 2026-10-16 (Asia/Jakarta), including purpose, boundaries/non-goals and all six success dimensions. This approval settles the **research/retrospective working baseline**, not operational Harscode policy or a change to main.

**Approved retrospective purpose (not operational/canonical Harscode policy):**

> Harscode is a foundation for Anhar to work with AI agents as competent, adaptive, trustworthy engineering partners. It should help understand and solve software engineering problems—whether bounded and clear or still ambiguous—through fit-for-purpose solutions, proportionate engineering rigor, visible reasoning and evidence, reliable continuity, and appropriate Human authority. Workflow, knowledge, specialization, state, and tools serve outcomes rather than constituting goals.

**Six approved retrospective success dimensions (qualitative; effectiveness remains to be validated):**

1. **Engineering quality:** correct, secure, maintainable, risk-appropriate outcomes with honest evidence.
2. **Fit and feasibility:** evaluate solution and execution feasibility; choose no more complexity than actual constraints demand.
3. **Effective pairing:** Anhar gains useful reasoning, challenge, practical execution, and judgment without becoming the perpetual dispatcher, prompt engineer, or process clerk.
4. **Visibility and trust:** clarify what is being done, why, what is proven/unproven, relevant risks, and precise Human decision points.
5. **Continuity and efficiency:** important settled intent/decisions/evidence reconstruct across agents/sessions while avoiding unnecessary rediscovery, rereading, and ceremony; current source still checked when material.
6. **Process health and proportionality:** engineering outcomes must be achieved without unjustified artifact proliferation, Runs/re-entries, redundant verification/context work, repetitive Human coordination, or non-converging loops. Judge cost against meaningful progress, new evidence, risk reduction, and necessary assurance—not raw activity counts. Harscode should be able to recognize and surface unhealthy patterns before reflexively continuing.

**Approved retrospective boundaries and non-goals:**

- Applicable to a narrow backend task, ambiguous investigation, or larger development effort; **no required end-to-end product pipeline**.
- Explore/Plan/Build/Review/Testing/PR is a useful engineering heuristic; **not a mandatory global phase sequence**.
- Orchestrator, Space, strict Work Unit/Run lifecycle, parallelism, workflow automation, dashboard, and particular storage layout are **optional hypotheses/implementation mechanisms**, not intrinsic purpose. A problem with no matching Space remains valid Agent Pairing input.
- Specialist profiles and non-LLM tools may help when task needs them; neither is mandatory or yet proven as a universal mechanism.
- Simplicity is constrained by real correctness, security, authority, independent verification where warranted, and reconstructability. Less documentation or fewer Runs alone are **not** success metrics. Conversely, an eventually correct result does **not** automatically excuse disproportionate process cost.
- **Process-health diagnostic is a desired capability, not an agreed dedicated workflow, dashboard, scoring system, mandatory audit Run, or monitoring platform.** Relevant signals include duplicative artifacts, repeated low-informational-delta Runs, recurring causal loops, excessive Human interventions, and unnecessary rediscovery/reverification. Interpret them with task context and risk; avoid treating a high count as proof of failure.
- Harscode owns reusable collaboration/engineering guidance; the target project retains its own product/design/security/implementation truth.

**Material tensions to test later, not decide at Station 1:** guidance depth versus LLM cognitive load; agent autonomy versus authority/safety; durable continuity versus artifact proliferation; collaboration benefit versus coordination cost; outcome correctness versus process burden. Diagnose process health proportionately **without creating a self-defeating diagnostic ceremony**. Existing main guidance and pilot ideas must be evaluated by actual results, not protected by sunk cost or discarded based only on impressions.

**Station 1 exit:** Approved by Anhar, 2026-10-16. Purpose, six evaluation dimensions and boundaries are agreed for this retrospective. No architecture, implementation mechanism or operational Harscode guidance has been approved by this decision.

## 1B. Station 2 — Understand Human–Agent Pairing (scope proposal, not yet approved)

**Started:** 2026-10-16. **Progress:** IN DISCUSSION — scope clarification. This is a requirements/experience inquiry, not design of a new workflow or architecture.

**Core question:** Given a bounded or ambiguous engineering concern, what collaboration and Agent behavior would genuinely help Anhar reach a trustworthy, feasible outcome with proportionate process cost?

**Proposed inquiry boundaries:** (1) starting interaction/task ambiguity; (2) reasoning, challenge and fit-solution discovery; (3) Agent autonomy, Human authority, specialists and non-LLM tools; (4) visibility, durable continuity and process-health awareness; (5) useful completion, evidence and handoff.

**Representative situations, not mandatory phases:** (A) well-defined backend task; (B) ambiguous engineering investigation; (C) work requiring additional specialist/independent verification. Cases are probes for behavior, not full end-to-end implementation tests at this station.

**Expected minimal exit evidence:** One compact account of desired Agent behaviors across the representative cases, Human–Agent authority/intervention boundaries, visibility/continuity needs, and material unresolved questions. Avoid additional documents, run structures and fixed process stages unless demonstrated necessary.

**Explicit exclusions:** No audit of whether current main/pilot already satisfies the needs (Station 3); no Space, Orchestrator, Profile architecture or tool design (Station 4); no validation experiments (Station 5). No assumption that collaboration must involve coordination, parallelism or a predefined Space.

**Additional pairing concerns from Anhar (2026-10-16; research observations, not approved policy):**

- Communication should be natural but use a concise, readable and sufficiently consistent decision-summary format when discussing material problems. Preserve problem, relevant evidence, tradeoffs, recommendation, decisions and next actions without ceremonial headings on every message.
- Human should supply task-appropriate inputs and prerequisites (requirements, bug symptoms, constraints and material approvals). Agent should challenge insufficient or contradictory inputs before execution. An unclear problem remains a valid starting point for investigation.
- Avoid material planning or redesign during an execution assignment. Distinguish ordinary implementation judgment from discovery of an unresolved material contract, authority, security, or architectural choice. The latter warrants a scoped stop and owner decision; it should not be resolved silently within execution.
- Need to investigate whether communication and readiness expectations should differ by task type, without prescribing one universal form, session split or new workflow mechanism.

**Additional pairing concern: scoped Human residual-risk acceptance (2026-10-16; research input, not approved implementation):**

- Anhar expects Agent to carry out planned execution and risk-proportionate verification honestly, but not all imperfect results must be rejected automatically. Authorized Humans may accept residual risk and permit a specifically bounded next step/delivery when they understand the consequences.
- Keep **technical verification outcome**, **owning-authority risk/requirement decision**, and **permission to progress/release** distinct. Example: 7 of 10 tests pass does not prove a 70% safety level; characterize each remaining case as FAIL / NOT RUN / INCONCLUSIVE / DEFERRED based on evidence, and determine material risk and blocking scope individually.
- Anhar's characterization of a finding as "minor" is input for challenge, not proof. Agent should surface concrete failure impact, security/privacy/data-integrity implications, uncertainty, alternatives and recommended decision. An authorized Human may accept residual risk but cannot turn failed/unperformed checks into technical PASS or override boundaries governed by another authority, law, mandatory compliance or still-unsatisfied HARD conditions. If approved acceptance criteria must change, reconcile the owning contract through valid authority rather than claiming the original condition was met.
- Preserve the bounded decision, owner, affected claim/delivery boundary, remaining failure/evidence gap, rationale and follow-up ownership in minimally sufficient durable state. Do not create a universal risk score or obligatory ceremony from this research observation.
- Existing reference, **not automatic solution**: operational main Testing supports `Pass with flagged follow-ups` and explicit blocking versus nonblocking findings; experimental pilot Proposal 0042 separates evidence reuse, deferral, authority waiver/contract change and Human residual-risk acceptance. The remaining research question is whether Human–Agent Pairing makes this usable without excessive process overhead.

**Clarification agreed in conversation (2026-10-16): high-risk pushback when Human wants to ignore a finding**

- Agent must actively flag high-consequence risk and challenge a request to ignore it with specific evidence, scope, uncertainty, plausible consequences and safer options. Strength of pushback should scale with severity and irreversibility.
- An owning Human may make a documented, scoped residual-risk acceptance within legitimate authority; this does not turn failing/absent evidence into PASS, authorize other delivery boundaries, or override legal/project/external requirements. Once a legitimate informed decision is settled, do not repeatedly reopen it without materially new evidence.

### Case A — Clear Engineering Task: working pairing expectations (2026-10-16)

**Research status:** Example behavioral hypothesis for Station 2. Not a canonical workflow, input template, approval gate, or implementation design.

**Representative task:** Update a backend API validation defect against existing applicable spec/contract.

**Desired collaboration:**
1. Human communicates intended change and points to task/project context, constraints and expected acceptance (directly or via durable authority). Agent should not demand repeating discoverable information.
2. Agent inspects relevant current repo authority/code and checks whether execution prerequisites are sufficiently settled; when material facts conflict/missing, explain what decision is needed. A task can be investigated even if it is not implementation-ready.
3. If ready, Agent executes the agreed scope using ordinary local implementation judgment and appropriate tests, without silently redoing product/architecture/security/contract planning. If a material premise is invalidated, stop the affected part and return the ownership decision.
4. Agent communicates concise outcome, changes, actual verification results, open gaps, residual risks and any precise Human decision. Avoid an extra artifact when already durable evidence suffices.
5. If Human seeks to proceed with imperfect verification, Agent assesses each failed/unknown check by consequence and accepted scope, pushes back especially on severe risks, separates evidence from scoped Human acceptance and delivery authorization, and preserves only necessary durable decision/evidence pointers.

**What we are seeking to learn:** the minimum Human input/Agent readiness discussion and meaningful outcome reporting for a bounded task; what local autonomy means; when independent verification/specialization is actually needed; and how to notice process cost without adding ceremony. No requirement for named workflow phases, a separate planning Session, Orchestrator, a Space, or an automatic profile.

**Case A status:** First pass documented; not yet validated or explicitly signed off by Anhar. Case B (ambiguous investigation) is the next contrasting situation after discussion.

### Case B — Ambiguous Engineering Problem: working pairing expectations (2026-10-16)

**Research status:** First-pass collaboration hypothesis under Station 2, not an approved workflow, Space, universal input form, mandatory Session split, or implementation plan.

**Representative example:** Human observes sporadic donation API latency and asks for investigation/recommendation without authorizing a code change. Root cause, solution and implementation acceptance criteria are not yet known.

**Expected Human contribution:** Describe known symptoms and business/engineering concern, available evidence pointers, environmental constraints and allowed access/actions where known. Not knowing root cause or solution is valid; Human should not have to invent those facts to invoke pairing. Clarify material missing information when the Agent cannot obtain it independently.

**Expected Agent behavior:**
1. Interpret the actual problem and desired help before assuming implementation. Use available repository/project knowledge and accessible evidence; ask only questions whose answers matter to safe next action.
2. Separate **observations / verified evidence / competing hypotheses / unknowns / proposed next diagnostic step**. Do not imply an unverified diagnosis is fact.
3. Act proactively within authorized, risk-proportionate investigation scope. A read-only or safely bounded technical diagnostic may be appropriate before the solution is known; production access, sensitive-data exposure, destructive writes or broader changes need applicable authorization.
4. Narrow uncertainty using the smallest useful inspection, reproduction, profiling or experiment. Consider consequences and simpler alternatives before suggesting a fashionable fix. Bring material product/contract/architecture/security choices to their owning authority.
5. Communicate concise, reconstructable findings and direction with evidence pointers, confidence limits, risks, recommendations and exact Human decision if required, not exhaustive narration or a default Techplan.
6. Know when to stop: evidence is sufficient for the next legitimate decision; a targeted experiment is identified; a genuine blocker requires the Human; or more identical work has weak expected informational delta. Avoid endless reruns, duplicated artifacts, repeated discovery and needless specialist escalation.
7. Keep **investigation**, **material solution decisions**, and **authorized implementation** semantically distinct, without requiring separate Sessions or artifacts for each. Investigation may include technical actions but is not implicit permission for a production code change. When the solution becomes implementation-ready, verify prerequisites/authority before implementing.
8. If a high-consequence risk is discovered, highlight it promptly and push back proportionately on suggestions to ignore it. Preserve technical evidence separately from any legitimately scoped Human residual-risk acceptance.

**What is not yet proven:** Which minimal input/readiness/summary conventions give the best LLM performance, and when a specialized agent or non-LLM diagnostic tool is beneficial. Station 3 audits existing main/pilot fit; Station 4 evaluates mechanisms.

**Case B status:** Working first pass recorded; awaiting critique from Anhar. Case C (additional specialist/independent perspective) remains to discuss.

### Case C — Specialist & Independent Perspective (2026-10-16, working research)

Representative example: backend donation settlement requires technical concurrency analysis, assuming the relevant product behavior and scope are already settled upstream. This is a hypothetical case, not an implementation finding.

**Desired pairing:** Default to one capable Engineering Agent. Involve extra expertise only for a material capability gap or risk; use the smallest bounded consultation. Do not equate an expert opinion with independent verification. A fresh reviewer should verify important claims against current code and evidence rather than automatically agree with the implementer. Non-LLM tests and checks may provide stronger observable evidence than additional Agent discussions.

A specialist request should identify the exact question, applicable authority and code/evidence anchors. Return a concise finding, uncertainty, risk and next recommendation; avoid requiring complete rediscovery or producing parallel versions of project truth. The engineering owner integrates conclusions and presents Human with a decision-ready outcome, escalating conflicting material authority decisions rather than deciding product semantics.

**Process health:** Watch for excessive specialist calls, duplicated reading, repeated consultations with no material new evidence, and ownership confusion. More Agents are not a success metric. When Human seeks to accept a severe unverified risk, explain and challenge consequences, and keep actual test results distinct from an authorized, scoped risk decision.

**Open research question:** What specialist/independent perspective is genuinely necessary in each context without binding Harscode to Orchestrator, formal Spaces, fixed Profiles, or a team workflow? Cases A/B/C remain working hypotheses, not approved procedures. The next Station 2 activity is to synthesize Engineering entry expectations and the Pre-engineering handoff boundary. No pilot before completion of relevant upstream Pre-engineering.

**Additional Case C concerns raised by Anhar (2026-10-16; research input):**

- Excessive active knowledge/guidance may impose context cost and increase risks from irrelevant or conflicting assumptions. Distinguish the model's general capability from the amount of task-time material loaded; greater background knowledge alone is not proof of greater bias.
- Determine useful specialization granularity empirically. Candidate default: a reusable, evidence-backed capability such as Go Backend Engineer, with narrowly routed concurrency/database guidance only when the actual concern triggers it; do not create a separate concurrency expert Profile solely because one task touches concurrency.
- Require a distinct specialist only when capability, risk, independence or recurring need materially justifies additional context transfer/coordination cost. Too narrow a profile taxonomy can itself create work and bias.
- Do **not** load all best-practices documents to make an Agent or Profile seem qualified. Load minimum stable role/project guidance plus the specific applicable concern guidance and live evidence. Preserve essential security/authority detail when actually triggered.
- This is a candidate approach, not an approved Profile architecture. In Station 3, compare it with existing main `best-practices/AGENTS.md` conditional routing and experimental Profile Discovery's required-versus-routed guidance. In real-use validation, test correctness, context load and handoff cost, rather than assuming less context is always better.

## 3. Human intent and constraints captured in retrospective

1. Prioritize intention, intended outcome, feasibility, and fit solution; technical detail remains appropriate when material, not the default center of gravity.
2. Desired experience is **Agent Pairing** (human–AI engineering partnership), not a mandatory dedicated Orchestrator product or workflow coordination engine. Multiple agents/parallelism are optional when useful.
3. No intent to build workflow automation or a strict engineering factory. Explore → Plan → Build → Review → Testing → PR is a useful heuristic, not an unconditional required chronology.
4. Fit-for-purpose, simplest sufficient robust solution; avoid fancy architecture and unnecessary ceremony, while retaining essential correctness/security safeguards.
5. Strong concern for **visibility** (what is happening, why, evidence, Human decisions) and **feasibility** (can the problem/solution actually be executed under current constraints).
6. Reconstructable durable state should reduce unnecessary cross-session/agent rediscovery and redundant document/codebase learning, without replacing necessary inspection of live source and authority.
7. Harscode should work for a single backend task, ambiguous investigation, or broader work as needed; full product lifecycle is not compulsory.
8. Existing orchestration implementation may prove entirely unnecessary; do not preserve it for sunk-cost reasons. Still assess its individual useful capabilities on their own merits.
9. Existing main guidance and best practices may themselves impose excess LLM context/process load; their effectiveness needs comparative real-use evidence, not assumption.
10. Specialized Agent Profiles are a promising preference: find appropriate capabilities for a problem without mandating persistent personalities, premature Space fitting, or a readiness ceremony.
11. Non-LLM tools may be useful where they are simpler or more reliable; avoid building a tooling platform without evidence.
12. A Space, if ever useful, should arise from a genuine bounded input/output responsibility. **An ambiguous or unmatched problem must still be serviceable by Agent Pairing without a pre-existing Space.**
13. (2026-10-16 clarification) Correct engineering outcomes must be **balanced with healthy, proportionate execution**. Harscode should recognize potentially excessive artifacts, Runs, re-entry, redundant verification, repetitive Human coordination, and similar process overhead; do not mistake raw activity counts for diagnosis or assume the existing experimental Loop Health Audit mechanism is required.
