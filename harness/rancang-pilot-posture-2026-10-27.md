## What “conditionally pilotable” permits us to consider

A further **documentation-only, bounded, supervised research invocation** may be proposed without requiring a stand-alone comprehensive Harness certification exercise first, if the actual assignment has proportionate evidence and a separate explicit Human go-ahead.

For each new pilot, the responsible Human/Coordinator must still:
1. Resolve the **exact formed Space definition**, target project/ref, accepted inputs, output/state destinations, authorized actions, real runtime/tool scope, and material intended consumers.
2. Check the material R1–R8 binding facts **in that real environment** rather than assume ORG-01 mechanics persist. For the revised six-Agent definition, verify relevant knowledge/context and independently inspectable original/producer outputs.
3. Keep **supervised, low-blast-radius, documentation-scoped** actions within explicit permitted paths. If native write scope is broader, disclose the specific R2 gap and request a **new, task-scoped Human risk decision**; never inherit the ORG-01 deviation. Project hard protections remain binding.
4. Preserve **independent Security and Solution findings**, exact source/output revisions, project-owned durable state, truthful `PARTIAL/UNVERIFIED/NOT RUN` labels and consequence-based Human gates. Missing native enforcement is not an assurance PASS.
5. Stop the **affected action**, rather than all unrelated work, if a mandatory safeguard cannot be honored, a protected/privileged write is needed, material authority changes, or the risk exceeds the explicitly authorized pilot scope.

This posture **does not authorize any invocation by itself** or permit feature code, migrations, production secrets, protected security/identity changes, deployments, autonomous execution or an unattended workflow. Those require their own authority and an appropriately stronger runtime assessment.
