## Hard rules

- `techplan/` protected files (`template.md`, `rules.md`,
  `guardrails.md`, `guidelines.md`, `diagram-guidelines.md`): never edit
  directly. Propose via `techplan/proposals/`.
- `techplan/examples.md` and `techplan/retro.md`: the one exception —
  append directly, no proposal needed.
- `exploration/`, `code-review/`, `testing/`, `pull-request/`: no
  protected files today, corrected in the moment. Don't invent guardrails
  or a proposal requirement here on your own judgment.
