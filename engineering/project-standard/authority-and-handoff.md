# Engineering Project Standards — Authority & Consumer Handoff

> **Standing:** A4 RESEARCH DRAFT / HUMAN CONTENT REVIEW PENDING. Real technical standards, accepted exceptions and exact project approval belong to the target project.

## Authority and precedence

The current **owning project artifact** and legitimate delegated Human/technical authority determine which reusable Engineering rule is effective and for what scope. A discovered note, architectural precedent, generated code, draft API, project index or old pilot does not become policy by existence.

Product owns accepted domain meaning, UX owns experience/IA/webpage routes, Rancang owns bounded technical solution proposals, actual implementing/canonical owners adopt feature API/DB schemas, and Project Engineering Standards owns **reusable technical rules**. An affected project standard cannot override higher-priority Product commitments, critical security/privacy protections, legal obligations, protected permissions or consequential Human gates. Resolve material conflicts with exact owner sources, not a silent Space-local preference.

## Change and acceptance

- **No change:** point to effective accepted rule or justified absence/decision freedom; no new approval machinery.
- **Local Engineering decision:** retain inside the bounded solution unless a real cross-feature constraint warrants promotion.
- **Project-wide rule:** show repeated-use evidence or justified current/committed project-wide need; proposed text, intended scope, affected known consumers, alternatives, security/privacy and compatibility effects. Require the owning authority's exact acceptance.
- **Supersession:** identify old and new effective versions, scope, migration/rollback if material, and actual consumer joins; preserve historical approved source identities.
- **Authorized persistence:** approval alone does not write; a new file or push alone does not approve. Reopen actual effective project original after permitted write, and distinguish ACCEPTED, PROPOSED, superseded and OPEN decisions.

Material security-control or privacy standards receive appropriately independent analysis, including risks to negative/abuse cases; the security decision owner—not the Architect's feasibility judgment—owns consequential risk exceptions where allowed.

## Consumer-facing contract

A Rancang, Builder or another actual consumer must be able to find: (a) owning project/branch/source and exact version, (b) binding scope and applicability/exceptions, (c) what freedoms remain local to the consumer, (d) actual evidence/assurance limits, (e) affected old/superseded versions and relevant incompatibilities, (f) unresolved material owner decisions.

Check **generic information fit** separately from **actual project-bound compatibility**. Unknown future consumers remain NOT CHECKED. A Harscode folder is not the project's source of truth and does not impose a mandatory standards folder or documentation format.
