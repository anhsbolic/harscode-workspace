# Experience & Craft Specialist — Applied Craft Knowledge

> **Agent-owned, non-binding** execution guidance. Target-project Product, Design, Brand, Asset Governance, Content, Accessibility and approved prototype standing always take precedence. This reference is not a universal design system or a mandate to generate images.

## Intentional craft (Anti-Slop)

Ask of every material visual/content choice:
- **Purpose:** does it improve navigation, understanding, trustworthiness, comprehension or the intended experience?
- **Authority:** is its meaning/brand/asset standing actually accepted or an ordinary delegated presentation choice?
- **Content truth:** do iconography, imagery, testimonials, badges and copy imply stronger verified status, permission or result than the system supports?
- **Craft:** is hierarchy, spacing, typography, responsive behavior, accessible labeling/focus and state presentation deliberate and robust?
- **Necessity:** could a simpler, purposeful presentation communicate better, without filler cards, gratuitous gradients, arbitrary illustration or icons?

No subjective “looks AI-made” veto; actual mismatch with applicable authority and consequences matters. A generated asset may be valid if it is appropriate, rights/provenance and approval are sufficient. A human-produced asset can still be slop. Don't treat project-specific brand decisions (e.g. Kencleng visual palette or icon choice) as NextJS Builder universal rules.

## Early collaboration and actual visual examination

- For material UI, surface information priority, consequential action/result meaning, content states and real accessibility/responsiveness needs before Engineer makes costly composition decisions. Discuss technical feasibility and cost in both directions; no mandatory Craft-first deliverable.
- Evaluate rendered screens at smallest representative content, state and viewport combinations able to reveal failures: long text, missing media, validation errors, pending/unknown result, sticky actions, narrow containers, keyboard/focus. Static code inspection cannot certify layout.
- Compare against accepted design **intent**, not forced pixel-perfect screenshots when technical tokens/version or documented prototype defects differ.
- State observable defects, consequences and correction, not “feels generic”; escalate unresolved consequential design direction to the owning authority rather than inventing a new brand.

## Assets and content when tools are limited

1. Locate existing approved project assets and licensed utility icon baseline.
2. Test whether the asset is necessary. Purposeful whitespace, text and layout often beat invented generic imagery.
3. If permitted tools and rights exist, create/explore a candidate with **noncanonical standing** until owning approval.
4. If tools cannot generate/edit/search an essential asset, provide a precise brief/prompt or sourcing specification and name missing Human/asset owner action; keep unrelated implementation moving.
5. Never silently fabricate canonical logo/wordmark, invented documentary photography or unsupported trust/verification icons. Respect privacy/licensing and distinguish placeholder, exploratory, proposed and approved artifacts.
