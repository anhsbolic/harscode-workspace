# Visual Composition, Asset Governance, Icons, and Copy

> **Applicable when:** composition, branding, illustration, iconography, typography, microcopy, trust communication or new assets are relevant. A project may have no formal brand book; absence creates ordinary expression freedom, not license to fabricate canonical identity.

## Intentional Craft

For each material choice evaluate **purpose, authority, content truth, craft and necessity**. Prefer useful hierarchy, whitespace, typography, contrast and clear labels to decorative cards, gratuitous gradients, generic illustrations or unsupported “verified” marks. Style should match effective Design intent rather than a universal aesthetic. AI provenance alone does not decide acceptability: review actual quality, accuracy, license, consent, provenance and approved status.

Good copy identifies what the actor is doing, what is known, what happens next and where uncertainty remains. Avoid empty impact slogans, vague CTA labels, false guarantees, raw system errors and manipulation. Legal/product-sensitive assertions need owning authority; Craft cannot invent a new external grant or status.

## Asset sourcing and production under tool limits

1. Discover the project's approved logo, wordmark, icon set, images, tokens and asset governance—including restricted use and derivative rights.
2. Decide whether a new asset is genuinely necessary. Many pages need no illustration; a functional utility icon should follow the project's approved family.
3. If the permitted tool can produce a candidate, distinguish exploratory/generated from approved and inspect for false depiction, sensitive content, permission or brand misuse.
4. If no suitable tool or license exists, supply a **concrete asset brief**: purpose, context, desired composition, dimensions/use, accessibility alt-text intent, source/licensing considerations, what needs Human/Brand approval. Do not hide the dependency with placeholder stock imagery.
5. Integrate into production only through Engineer's accountable implementation owner and project approval/usage rules. If essential asset cannot be secured, hold the specific dependent claim; continue independent work.

## Review signals

Look for hierarchy reversed on mobile, irrelevant decoration that competes with an important action, unapproved canonical-looking logos, inaccessible image-only controls, inconsistent typography, unrealistic photographs presented as documentary proof, or copy claiming complete success from unknown backend results. Report the actual surface/source/consequence and a smallest viable correction.
