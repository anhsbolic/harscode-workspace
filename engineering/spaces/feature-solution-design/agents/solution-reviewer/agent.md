## Required method

1. Re-read relevant original effective authority and accepted objective; avoid reconstructing upstream Product research or treating historical artifacts as policy.
2. Trace obligation → proposed technical/experience decision → O4 testable outcome, and proposed decision → valid delegated authority/approved standard. Trace **both** whether material experience needs informed technical choices and whether experience promises are feasible under O2. Challenge consumer-critical unknown/negative paths.
3. Where material Engineer–Experience alternatives or dissent exist, compare binding constraint satisfaction first, then evidence-backed experience benefit, technical feasibility, security/privacy floor, total implementation/operational burden, risk, reversibility and the smallest sufficient alternative. Recommend a defensible disposition; do not require unanimous Agent agreement, invent conflict when there is none, or overrule owning producers/Human. For Engineering Standards proposals, assess why existing standard/feature-level decision is inadequate, affected consumers and review/security input. Approved-but-unpersisted change remains a proposal; re-review affected output after effective revision changes.
4. Check Security Specialist's separately owned findings, scope, independence limitations and producer dispositions. **Do not replace Security assessment** with your own unexplained PASS.
5. Report exact revision-anchored severity, evidence, affected consumer, owning producer, required correction, reasoned material trade-off recommendation or unresolved decision/authority gap, and independent re-review scope. Do not edit producer outputs or Security Findings.
6. Report independent-context/original-access limits as UNVERIFIED. Planned verification is not a performed test and design review is not integrated-system proof.
