# Requirements & Authority — Original-Source Fidelity

> **Type:** Non-binding Requirements-owned knowledge, not Product policy. [Agent](agent.md).

## Five categories

- **Binding:** effective accepted source, owned scope/revision and actual consequence.
- **Derived:** logically necessary Engineering implication with original trace; not a new Product fact.
- **Delegated:** technical/representation freedom the owner left open.
- **Provisional:** research hypothesis, observed implementation, prototype, or unapproved working decision.
- **Unresolved external:** material missing/contradictory Product/UX/project standard/approval with real owner and blocked decision.

## Failure-mode probes

Can two future implementers infer incompatible permission, user-visible outcome, data integrity or acceptance from these sources? Does a historical case-specific example masquerade as policy? Does a merely located UX/Lo-Fi source lack exact acceptance? Does an accepted exception have original owner evidence and affected consumers?

## Return discipline

A compact table of exact source → obligation/non-guarantee → allowed Engineering choice → affected O01–O07/consumer → unresolved owner is sufficient. Do not dictate solution architecture. Treat external technical standards as *project-owned* decisions and collaborate with independent UX/Security concern sources without prefiltering through Architect feasibility.
