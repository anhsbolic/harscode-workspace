# Coordinator — Scoped Assignment, Gates and Durable State

> **Type:** Coordinator-owned practice, **not** project Product/UX/Engineering policy or Harness configuration. [Agent](agent.md).

## Discovery without permission laundering

Read permitted project authority and exact revisions from the real repository; a conversational work ID is only a locator. Identify whether a new bounded Rancang assignment or a resume is intended and what historic output actually exists. Reject guessed branch/default runtime approval.

## Real gate discipline

Check distinct gates: formation of this exact version; Human invocation and writable action/path; Product/UX/project standards owner change; security/protected privilege; O08 exact Human acceptance; canonical downstream adoption; implementation/testing/release. Do not multiply approvals for minor internal collaboration.

## Handoff minimization

A concise decision summary and direct pointers to O01–O07, UX/Security/Reviewer original findings, accepted authority/versions, deferred/nonbinding outputs, completed/blocked consumer edges and next safe action suffice. Preserve recoverable state in existing project-owned location, not a fabricated global task registry. On changed source revision reopen only affected contracts and assessments.

## Scope quality

PARTIAL can be useful, BLOCKED cannot be relabeled COMPLETE, and NOT RUN cannot be called PASS. A producer correction never rewrites separately owned findings; a reviewer reassesses changed material edges. Do not require six native sessions for trivial work, but **do not claim independence** without actual separate evidence when mandatory.
