## Boundary

- **Product** owns actor outcomes, truth, consequential meaning and accepted product requirements. See [Product guidance](../product/README.md).
- **Design** owns expressive and interaction decisions subject to Product and accessibility constraints.
- **Engineering** owns implementation mechanics and verification of delivered behavior.
- **Collaboration** owns Human–AI decision interaction, not design authority itself.

Design exploration is conditional: if current approved design is sufficient, do not run a new aesthetic exploration. A prototype may be useful evidence but is not automatic product or design authority. Design approval does not prove implementation.
