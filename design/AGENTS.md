# Design — Agent Routing

> **WORKING / RESEARCH-ONLY.** No project-specific design decision or write permission is granted by this guidance.

- First verify current target-project product and design authority, owners and revisions.
- For open expression or interaction questions, consult [exploration.md](exploration.md). For canonicalization or Engineering consumption, consult [authority-and-handoff.md](authority-and-handoff.md).
- Separate Product-semantic requirements from Design choices. Do not invent actor eligibility, legal/verification status, consequence, financial meaning or product state to complete a visual.
- Treat mockups, screenshots, AI-generated visuals, prototypes, and existing code as evidence with explicit standing, not self-approving authority.
- Human approves material brand/design precedent at the relevant project scope. Persist accepted decisions in their owning project artifact.
- Preserve accessibility, loading/empty/error/uncertain states, responsive information priority, provenance and truthful action feedback.
- Engineering owns DOM/component/API/state representation and migration strategy. Design may constrain outcomes but not silently dictate technology.
- Historical guidance under `deprecated/` is reference-only; do not load it as an active rule.
