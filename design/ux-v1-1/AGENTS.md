# AGENTS — UX v1.1 Experimental Execution

> **Standing:** EXPERIMENTAL instructions usable **only when Human explicitly selects this research UX framework for a bounded assignment**. This file does not override repository-root instructions, protected-write boundaries, approved project authority or the separately accepted `design/ux/` concern. Not a Space invocation contract.

## Required bounded behavior

1. **Verify and classify.** Identify the actual user-facing problem, affected actors/decisions, outcome, legitimate owner, approved project Product/UX/Design Standards sources at exact revisions, relevant accessibility/device/privacy/security/technical constraints, and current evidence. Label accepted facts, hypotheses and missing decisions separately. Never interpret working research as Product truth.
2. **Challenge the problem, not just the format.** Ask what the relevant person is trying to accomplish, what they may misunderstand, what consequences matter, and whether the inherited workflow is actually necessary. Reuse good existing decisions for their merits, not because old screens must survive.
3. **Discover proportionally.** Identify high-value unanswered actor questions and evidence gaps; use existing data where sufficient. Do not invent interviews, quotes, needs or statistical validation. Missing evidence blocks only materially dependent binding decisions; explicitly provisional safe exploration may proceed.
4. **Conceptualize and decide.** Define an actor-facing mental model expressing accepted Product meaning. Challenge at least the material alternative or counterexample when warranted, then choose one strong proposed concept. Sketching may precede formal concept writing; no mandatory linear sequence.
5. **Express the experience, not a document checklist.** Make the relevant task and relationship concepts, interaction branches, information structure, navigation, actions, states and recovery inspectable using appropriate representations. For **new/material screen UX**, provide inspectable flow/branch proof and representative structural Lo-Fi by default for receiving UI; preserve applicable accessibility and negative/uncertain states. Existing accepted equivalents or a scoped documented non-applicability/unchanged decision can meet obligations. No fixed format for all media.
6. **Evaluate honestly.** Define what a real actor would need to understand/do and what would falsify the design. Inspect actual layouts/interaction where possible; ask Human to review; capture criticism, revise or state outstanding risks. Never call visual self-review an actor usability test, accessibility certification or real backend evidence.
7. **Reconcile authority and consumer meaning.** Return truly missing Product semantics to Product with a concrete implication/recommendation; technical facts to Engineering; reusable standards to their legitimate owner. UX may decide routine delegated design choices. Material UX acceptance needs legitimate Human/exact-source project evidence. Keep the accepted legacy UX/UI O2/O6 compatibility issue explicit, not silently mapped by numeric IDs.
8. **Deliver usable review material.** Provide a small, complete set of inspectable output links and a concise decision/evidence note. Distinguish `PROPOSED` from `ACCEPTED`, `CHECKED` from `NOT CHECKED`, and desired quality from observed user outcomes. Preserve a reconstructable source/decision trail.

## Stop / do not claim

- Do **not** invent rights, legal identity, Product success, payment effects, privacy claims, external approval, available user actions, backend capabilities or navigable routes.
- Do **not** convert candidate mental-model graphics into verified domain authority, or claim that sign-in/one successful substep completes a multi-part Product outcome.
- Do **not** replace the effective project UX or public Harscode UX guidance merely by using this folder. No protected writes, Space dispatch, production, formal approval or release is implied.
- Do **not** require a new multi-file packet, diagram count, user-test ceremony or prototype when the actual decision and consumer can be served more simply.
- Do **not** finalize a dependent UI/Engineering acceptance if material flow, representative structure, state, actual authority or effective source is missing or unverified.
