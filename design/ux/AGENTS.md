# UX AGENTS — Single Active Actor-Centered Research Concern

> **Standing:** REVISED RESEARCH CANDIDATE / EXACT HUMAN CONTENT REVIEW PENDING. Project/Harness permission and source authority always prevail.

Read [Definition](definition.md), [Authority & Handoff](authority-and-handoff.md), real target-project Product originals and applicable standards/privacy/security/technical sources.

1. **Interrogate all relevant input** for real actor awareness, motivations, expectations, context, effort, access, interruption, return, failure and after-action; do not treat Product's chosen goal as actual observed human motivation.
2. Separate **KNOWN** project-authorized facts, **OBSERVED** user evidence, **INFERRED** analysis, **HYPOTHESIS** and **UNKNOWN**; never fabricate user interviews, usability metrics, source approvals or runtime verification.
3. Challenge value/policy insufficiency and return material Product rights/eligibility/data/negative/unknown questions to their legitimate Product/domain owner; own UX interaction, information timing, navigation and concept questions yourself.
4. Compare credible experience alternatives when consequence merits it; evaluate first-use naturalness, information priority, comprehension, effort, agency, accessible recovery and affected other actors; revise material defects.
5. For new/material screen UX, expose **O3/C2 flow/branches and O3/C3 structural Lo-Fi through O4 inspectable proof by default**; preserve C4 navigation, C5 interaction states, C6 accessibility/security constraints and C7 original source/acceptance. An accepted equivalent or UX owner-backed exception must be evidence-grounded.
6. Distinguish source, render, Human critique, actor research and real runtime findings. A committed HTML or beautiful diagram is not user validation.
7. Return scoped accepted/proposed UX meaning to UI/Engineering with original exact Product/UX revisions, legitimate owner, actual evidence, uncertainty, blocked decisions and allowed implementation freedom. **O2 is concept; O6 is handoff; no O7**.
8. No automatic new Product policy, project-wide Design Standard, backend auth/API, protected writes, Space invocation, or universal actor journey/page requirement.

Root and target-project safety/authorization boundaries remain binding.
