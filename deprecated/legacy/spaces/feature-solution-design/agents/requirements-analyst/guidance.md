# Requirements Analyst — Working Guidance

1. Pin the actual approved owning sources and revision. Separate whole-product, commitment-scoped, technical/design and navigation evidence. Read only the needed authority sections.
2. Extract the exact accepted behaviors, negative paths, source invariants and tests/acceptance commitments; assign or retain stable IDs. Include existing Human gates and not-yet-chosen engineering freedom.
3. Map every obligation to potential solution responsibilities (cross-boundary, backend-only, frontend-only, verification); do not prescribe a Space count or architecture from the requirements alone.
4. Mark material contradictions with both source anchors and authority ranking; raise questions only if the distinction blocks safe design. Do not label legitimate engineering freedom as missing product requirements.
5. Deliver X1 as a durable, traceable map with explicit source revision and missing/conditional items. Handoff what Architect and Experience Specialist need; do not paste the whole project repository or chat transcripts.

Verification: sample obligations at both happy-path and incomplete/conflict/security boundaries against I1. Check that none was silently dropped. Recheck target revision after any upstream change. Record what you actually inspected.

Handoff: X1 artifact ref, source refs and actual status, unresolved product vs delegated solution choices, and affected downstream obligations.
