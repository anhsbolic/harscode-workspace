## Method and inspection coverage

1. Inventory the **entire source checkpoint tree** from the Git tree with `recursive=1`; distinguish foundation sources, experiment sources and history.
2. Source-review foundation architecture/entrypoints and relevant Product phases/templates, Design guidance, Engineering Space formation, Rancang and NextJS Builder external I/O, relevant Agent/coordination/artifact guidance, approval records, and Harness/Collaboration authority boundaries.
3. Compare **ownership, semantic coverage, consumer fit and approval standing** against the candidate model, **not document counts or matching folder names**.
4. Mark uninspected or unproven edges honestly. This is a **first-pass repository-wide structural inventory with targeted source-depth checks**, not a line-by-line certification of every file, exhaustive Agent-knowledge/security review, or verified runtime assessment.

## How to interpret the differences

1. **Five candidate concerns vs six Product phases:** not an error in itself. The existing `PROD-FND / PROD-JRN / PROD-SEL / PROD-DEF / PROD-APR / PROD-HND` are Product-internal activities and typed outputs. Their semantics can feed the broader candidate Product logical outputs. Do **not** collapse or expand merely to make the counts match.
2. **Thirteen candidate Rancang inputs vs four external classes:** the candidate describes **information needs**; the current external contract groups sources by acquisition and authority. The question is whether a **materially required UX interaction/route contract is reliably located, checked for standing, and reconciled**. Compare that behavior, not table length.
3. **Ten experiment Builder inputs vs NextJS Builder's five classes:** one is an **actor-outcome full-stack example**, the other a reusable **frontend implementation capability** with known ownership boundaries. Cross-system integration/acceptance belongs to the actual project owner, not necessarily a newly formed Space.
4. **No mandatory Space for Lo-Fi or Hi-Fi:** the candidate says those are concerns/outputs, not necessarily reusable Spaces; current Architecture consciously scopes Spaces to Engineering. Missing Design guidance ≠ need to form Design Spaces.
5. **Product consequential information vs UX content:** Product should own **which information is domain-required**, while UX/Design owns **how it is organized, presented, and interacted with**. The candidate demands clarity of this boundary, not all final copy in the Product Contract.
6. **Approved definition versus later source labels:** current effective Human approval pins an exact original revision. A reader opening a contract directly may see original candidate labeling; review the approval anchor before calling the capability rejected or automatically approved at today's HEAD.

## What is not demonstrated by this review

- Not all 84 active files have been independently evaluated line-by-line for semantic consistency or conflict, and none of the 258 historical files was promoted into authority.
- No actual target-project Design, API, Product/Space consumer or runtime behavior was tested as part of **this** Harscode-only audit.
- No evidence yet that the 60 candidate sources exist with valid approvals in a specific project; no proof every arrow is necessary.
- No decision to amend formed Engineering Space definitions, alter Product or Design guidance, change mainline policy, or invoke a Space.
