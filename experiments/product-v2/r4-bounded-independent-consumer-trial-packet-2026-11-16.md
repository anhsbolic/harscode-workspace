## 2. Fixed synthetic Product fixture (SHOW to each consumer)

**Case:** A fictional community organizer offers a **free, single-session workshop** and allows a participant to request, inspect and cancel a reservation for one seat through a new web experience. There are **12 seats**. This is a hypothetical test, not a real organizer or institution.

**Fixed scenario Product commitments (test stipulations, not real owner approval):**

1. At any moment, a workshop may have **at most 12 active confirmed reservations**. A successful reservation is confirmed only once the authoritative reservation state reflects the accepted result. A request attempt or attractive UI is not confirmation.
2. Only **one active reservation per email address per workshop** is allowed. Email is used for necessary reservation/status/cancellation messages; it must not be displayed publicly.
3. The reservation holder may cancel their own active reservation. A canceled reservation no longer occupies a seat. A participant cannot cancel another person's reservation.
4. A reservation request may be **confirmed**, **denied** (for example full or duplicate), or **unknown** to the caller after timeout/ambiguous transport outcome. A timeout must not be represented as confirmation or definitive rejection unless the authoritative outcome has been established.
5. The offering is free. There is **no waitlist and no payment capability** in the bounded scope. Do not imply them through UI or technical designs.
6. Bounded goal: the relevant person can legitimately understand whether they currently hold a confirmed seat and can cancel it when entitled. No success may depend on exceeding capacity or guessing a prior unknown result.

**Important intentionally UNRESOLVED questions (return to actual concern owner rather than inventing binding policy):**

- What evidence/verification is required to establish that the actor controls the email address and is entitled to cancel or inspect a reservation? Product/domain authority defines entitlement policy; Engineering proposes secure proof/enforcement mechanisms under accepted rules, with independent Security/Privacy assessment.
- What are the effective email retention/deletion and communication consent requirements? Project privacy/legal owner needed if material.
- Are there time limits for reservation/cancellation, organizer overrides, or transfers? Not provided; **no silent rule**. A consumer may ask the Product test owner to resolve before binding related UX/technical choices.
- What real actor discovery channels and before/after motivations exist? **No actual user research or validated journey supplied**. UX should investigate or label hypotheses rather than assume someone is already motivated.

**Research-scope boundaries:** No external integration, backend, code, runtime, production project, permission grant, or actual user study is supplied. This fixture is not a license to exercise tools or make authoritative project claims. A proposed registration mechanism is a hypothesis until legitimate owner review.

**Risk triggers for consumer scrutiny:** Two people request the last seat concurrently; a participant retries after timeout; duplicate email submission; cancellation attempted by a nonholder; canceled seat freeing capacity; a UI falsely displaying a success from a request attempt; visually clear but misleading negative/unknown feedback.
