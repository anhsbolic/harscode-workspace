## 2. Consumer-critical sufficiency that must not disappear through aggregation

- **Product -> UX/UI/Engineering:** Consumer can reconstruct applicable value/scope, legitimate rights/eligibility, consequential effects and failure/unknown semantics, real prerequisites, observable acceptance, original owner/sources, accepted/provisional standing and exact revision. Four frozen Product v2 information groups are **not four mandatory physical files** and are **not yet proven universally sufficient**. An approved Product Contract document is one possible representation; direct accepted original sources or durable pointers can satisfy the need.
- **UX -> UI/Engineering:** Material interactions must expose concrete flow branches, IA/navigation/web route distinction where applicable, information priority/timing, actor-visible validation/denial/unknown/recovery and accessibility meaning. Preserve A2 **Flow Diagram O2** and **representative Lo-Fi O6 required-by-default** for new/material screen UX, with accepted exact-revision equivalents or evidence-backed owner exceptions. UX v1.1 may organize those meanings under different outputs, **not** by silently equating O2/O6 IDs.
- **UI -> Engineering:** When visual interface implementation is in scope, receiving Engineering needs effective screen and responsive composition, material state appearances, final scoped copy, necessary component/asset and provenance constraints, actual design acceptance and render/inspection limitations. Hi-Fi is **not** a universal prerequisite for Engineering-only feasibility or Rancang.
- **Engineering Solution -> actual implementer:** Do not conflate a readable prose proposal with a consumer-ready typed API contract when actual accepted project format requires typed/complete machine-readable semantics. Preserve identity, permission, data integrity, race/error/unknown and observability obligations, exact handoff evidence and independent Security/Solution review. Technical verification specifications are **NOT RUN** unless actually executed.
- **Feedback edges:** **UX -> Product** returns actor evidence/unmet needs/invalidated assumptions/recommended Product changes; **UI -> UX** returns presentation/interaction clarity and visual feasibility evidence/recommended UX corrections; Engineering returns feasibility/risk/real-world behavior. These are **evidence, not authority transfers**.
- **Direct owner inputs:** Security/Privacy, Project Design Standards, Engineering Project Standards and legitimate Human/domain authority are independently sourced and binding when material. Their requirements/assurance may not be replaced by one producing concern's summary or self-approval.
- **Decision-specific sufficiency:** Bounded research may proceed with labeled hypotheses; dependent binding decisions require applicable accepted original meaning, exact revision, legitimate owner and material proof. Missing information holds the **affected decision**, not the entire research graph.

This is a **proposal for bounded R3 verification**, not a license for a wholesale rewrite. No source's existing exact-revision approval is transferred to new hypothetical R2 mappings.
