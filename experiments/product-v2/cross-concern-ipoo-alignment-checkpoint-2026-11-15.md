## 4. Proposed research sequence (not an operational workflow)

### R1 — Zero-based responsibility and information boundary map

- Start from **frozen Product v2** and actual professional decision authority; define each concern's owned decisions, permitted discovery, input information, output meaning and evidence/approval boundary.
- Map *semantic information edges*, not mandatory phase handoffs: Product → UX, Product → UI/Engineering where consequential, UX → UI, UX → Engineering, UI → Engineering when visual constraints are material, Engineering ↔ Product/UX/UI feedback, and direct Security/Privacy/Design & Engineering Standards sources as applicable.
- Classify each edge as binding, provisional/research, conditional, inapplicable, or unresolved by actual decision; identify which original owning source and approval must be inspectable.
- **Output:** one compact boundary/information map, with concrete disputed ownership questions and risk/confidence. No new taxonomy merely to fill gaps.

### R2 — Derive candidate I/P/O/O across concerns

- Derive inputs, substantive processes, consumable outputs and desired outcomes from R1, independently of existing counts/templates.
- Inspect current Product, UX v1.1, UI and Rancang research definitions as **comparison evidence**, retaining already-justified safeguards. Classify each existing requirement **KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE / UNVERIFIED**, with source and consequence.
- Explicitly distinguish: exploratory UX readiness versus binding UX acceptance; bounded Engineering feasibility versus binding Engineering solution; semantics versus representation; approved Product meaning versus provisional hypothesis.
- **Output:** candidate cross-concern I/P/O/O (logical meanings, not mandatory files/stages) plus a minimal proposed compatibility delta, not changed live definitions.

### R3 — Compatibility and authority probes

- Inspect real consumer contract meanings and exact revisions, not only names/IDs. Check Product v2 → UX v1.1, Product truth → Engineering, UX v1.1 → UI, UX → Rancang, UI → Engineering and relevant feedback.
- Known example: **UX v1.1 output ID collision** (experimental O2/O6 meaning differs from old UX O2 Flow/O6 Lo-Fi consumed by UI/Rancang; Rancang I05 also refers to old O7). Do **not** invent or silently approve a new ID mapping.
- Test whether Engineering `I01 = Product Contract` actually requires a named document or **accepted, directly inspectable consequential Product meaning**; preserve exact-revision authority and observable acceptance.
- Identify which UX/UI inputs materially affect Engineering Solution Design and which are not applicable/needed only later; safeguard identity/security, data and visual/actor truth.
- **Output:** source-grounded edge dispositions and only material unresolved owner decisions; no faux consumer PASS.

### R4 — Independent bounded trial and evidence review

- Use one **project-independent** product scenario for applicable Product → UX → UI → Engineering source/consumer reasoning, separated by actual consumer contexts where claiming independence.
- Each consumer sees sufficient legitimate inputs with controlled exposure to author-side simulations; capture actual questions, outputs, source standing, limits, counterexamples and feedback.
- Test security/consequential failure/unknown paths and distinguish conceptual proof, accepted decisions, engineering feasibility evidence, independent review, executed verification and delivered human value.
- **Output:** trial/evidence report, exact limitations, one recommended candidate alignment design or clearly documented blockers.

R1–R4 are **research activities**, not universal product stages, not a fixed authorization sequence. Shorten or revisit steps only when evidence justifies it.

## 5. Evaluation and stop condition

Research is sufficiently complete for a *candidate alignment decision* when:

- Every material Product/UX/UI/Engineering decision has a clear legitimate owner and evidence/authority source.
- Input/output joins preserve binding Product semantics **without** turning UX/UI into mandatory Product-authority relays or requiring Engineering to guess them.
- UX can own actor inquiry and experience design without mandatory Product-authored user journey; Engineering can start safe permitted exploration before all Design evidence is ready, while binding decisions respect applicable Product, Design, technical/security authority.
- Meaning-based UX/UI/Rancang consumer contracts have been checked, including known ID collisions and original-source/approval standing.
- At least one independent bounded trial (or an explicitly limited research result) is available and its evidence standing is honestly stated.
- No unnecessary document counts, workflow engine, compulsory handoff ceremony, blanket approval or silent promotion has been introduced.
- Unresolved material ownership/conflicts, safety limits, current project consumer fit and runtime evidence are labeled instead of hidden.

**If material evidence is missing:** report the exact missing edge/owner and stop at the evidenced level; do not redefine Product v2's frozen baseline retrospectively.
