## 3. Concrete improvement candidates, when compatible with active validation

**Priority A — Harscode Product Definition P3/P4 (#3+#4):** One bounded generic clarification that (a) identifies the legitimate effective decision basis, (b) assesses whether relevant claims are adequately evidenced rather than minting ad-hoc criteria, and (c) checks which information is necessary at each material decision point. Preserve existing freedom of representation and decision-specific judgment. This is a **candidate**, not an approved replacement for current P3/P4.

**Priority B — Harscode Consumer Handoff §§5–6/10 (#2):** One illustrative distinction between bounded Product completion, conditional receiving use, and separately authorized implementation. An example must not become mandatory global status fields, a stage gate, or an automatic handoff.

**Priority C — owning project index (#7):** Shorten current Product authority routing **only after observing or protecting the active fresh-reader UX intake baseline**. Keep dated provenance accessible at original sources. This is a project-specific navigation maintenance change, not reusable Harscode semantic guidance.

**Investigation only (#6):** Before changing persistence advice, obtain process-level examples showing actual repeated approvals, needless checkout cycles, or semantic risk if a checkpoint is removed. Test a cheap reduction only where original source/decision/limitations remain reconstructable.

**Already covered (#1,#5,#8):** Use as case evidence for future comparison, not duplicated rules.
