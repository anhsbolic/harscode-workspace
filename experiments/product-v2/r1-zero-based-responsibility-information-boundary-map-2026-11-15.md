## 2. Decision ownership map

| Concern | Legitimately owned decisions | Permitted inquiry / consumable meaning | Must not silently decide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product** | Legitimate value commitments; target beneficiary/value scope; capability boundaries; rights, eligibility, obligations, policies, meaningful domain success/denial/unknown; product-level trade-offs and semantic acceptance | Investigate value and priorities; integrate UX/Engineering evidence; expose effective commitments, constraints, material unknowns, source/approval scope | UX journey/interaction, visual expression, mechanisms; decisions beyond delegated Human/domain authority |
| **UX** | Human experience problem framing; actor inquiry; experience concepts; interaction flow, IA, applicable navigation; information timing, feedback, agency, recovery and interaction accessibility | Investigate actual contexts, motivations, misunderstandings and unmet needs; produce inspectable flows/structural evidence when material; report Product gaps and design limits | Product promises, eligibility/policy, mandatory data requirements or unapproved capability; technical controls |
| **UI** | Scoped visual hierarchy/composition, responsive treatment, final bounded UI copy, assets, component variants and expression of accepted actor-facing states | Explore visual/presentation options, produce inspectable visual/copy/state evidence, and report unclear or infeasible UX meaning | Material Product/UX semantics, project-wide Design Standard promotion or Engineering mechanism |
| **Engineering** | Technical feasibility, architecture/implementation mechanisms, API/data/internal state, identity enforcement, resilience/recovery mechanisms, and technical verification | Investigate stack/integration/risk, proposed solutions, implementation evidence and technical feedback. **Rancang** owns the Engineering Solution design slice, not every Engineering task | Product/domain rights or UX navigation; unilateral security waiver, shared-contract canonical adoption or project authority |
| **Security / Privacy** | Independently owned protective constraints, risk/threat/privacy assessment and professional assurance within legitimate scope | Direct source-backed safeguards/challenges; findings and risk consequences | Architect's delegated mechanism by default, Product policy, or Human-only material risk acceptance |
| **Project Design / Engineering Standards** | Approved cross-feature/project-wide reusable design or technical rules, scope and supersession | Provide effective standards; assess whether a genuinely reusable new precedent is justified; \`reuse/no change\` is valid | Local feature Product/UX/UI/Engineering decisions absent legitimate delegation |
| **Human / Domain Authority** | Material decisions, delegated mandates, institutional/domain policy, consequential exceptions and risk acceptance according to actual authority | Exact decision, scope, approval/rejection and revision; may receive evidence from any concern | Automatic professional ownership of every routine design/technical choice or powers beyond law/policy |

**Interpretation:** Decision ownership, evidence contribution and final authorization are distinct. A discovering consumer may challenge and propose a correction, but does not inherit authority over the source's consequential meaning.

## 3. Minimum information edges — conditional, not a pipeline

| Semantic edge | Information needed or returned | Binding/provisional boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product -> UX** | Intended value, target scope/actors, supported capability, consequential rules/constraints, known uncertainty | Proposed meaning supports bounded discovery; relevant effective accepted Product meaning is needed to bind dependent UX decisions |
| **Product -> UI** | Exact consequential content/disclosures, permitted claims, rights and truthful result-state meaning | Direct Product original/source pointer when material; UX is not a Product-authority relay |
| **Product -> Engineering** | Accepted domain roles/eligibility, invariants, behavior, failure semantics, observable acceptance | Direct authoritative sources/pointers when material for binding solution; no mandatory Product Agent handoff or one named file |
| **UX -> UI** | Experience concept, flows, IA/navigation, structural Lo-Fi or equivalent where material, interaction states, recovery/accessibility | Affected binding UI decisions use accepted scoped UX meaning; provisional visual study remains nonbinding |
| **UX -> Engineering** | Accepted user-facing states, entry/exit, actor interaction/recovery and navigation constraints when they affect technical choices | Applicable to dependent design/implementation; not a blanket precondition for technical-only feasibility |
| **UI -> Engineering** | Relevant accepted visual, responsive, copy/asset and component/state treatment | Applies when implementation depends on visual constraints; not a default prerequisite for backend feasibility |
| **Engineering -> Product** | Feasibility/cost, technical risk, observed behavior, contradictions, alternatives and Product consequences | Evidence/feedback only; consequential Product change returns to Product/domain owner |
| **Engineering -> UX/UI** | Realizable constraints, technical options, runtime/implementation observations, inconsistency affecting experience or expression | Evidence/feedback only; UX/UI owners decide relevant revised design meaning |
| **UX -> Product** | **Actor evidence, unmet needs, invalidated assumptions and recommended Product changes** | **Feedback edge; not authority transfer.** Product/domain owner accepts/revises consequential commitments with appropriate approval |
| **UI -> UX** | **Presentation evidence, interaction clarity issues, visual feasibility and recommended experience corrections** | **Feedback edge; not authority transfer.** UX owner decides material accepted interaction/IA change; consequential Product impact escalates further |
| **Security/Privacy -> affected concerns** | Original protective requirements, independently assessed risk, privacy/data constraints and mitigation challenges | Binding as applicable under owning authority; exceptions/risk acceptance require legitimate Human/domain authorization |
| **Project Standards -> affected concerns** | Effective reusable project Design/Engineering constraints and scope | Original owner/revision applies; proposed feature precedent is not a project-wide rule |
| **Human/Domain -> affected concerns** | Delegation, accepted policy/decision, scoped exceptions and exact approval | Only binding for actual owner's mandate, scope and accepted revision |

Edges describe **semantic information access and feedback**, not required producer order, dispatch graph, global lifecycle, additional mandatory documents or new Space topology.

## 4. Decision-specific readiness, authority and durable-state checks

- **Exploratory:** Provisional Product/Design ideas can support explicitly bounded inquiry and feasibility. Do not present hypotheses as effective rights, promises, accepted design or working system behavior.
- **Binding:** Consumer must obtain **sufficient original accepted meaning for that particular decision**—Product, UX, UI, security/privacy and project-standard inputs as applicable—with legitimate owner, scope, effective revision and relevant evidence. A discoverable source pointer can suffice; a named handoff packet is not inherently authoritative.
- **Blocked edge:** If missing meaning changes rights, safety, privacy, obligations or material experience, hold the **dependent binding claim**, not all unrelated inquiry. Return exact question, sources inspected, impact, proposed answer and correct owner.
- **Evidence != approval != runtime:** Human Product approval, exact UX/UI acceptance, Rancang formation, proposed Engineering Solution, canonical API/schema adoption, executed technical verification and delivered user value are distinct. Independent security/reviewer assurance must not be inferred from role labels or one shared context.
- **Fresh-session reconstruction test:** An affected consumer must be able to inspect effective meaning, original owning source, exact revision, approval scope, material unknowns and unresolved owner without ChatGPT Memory. This is a test of sufficient information, **not** a mandated seven-field document template or central registry.

## 5. Material contested boundaries and preliminary R1 dispositions

1. **Product actor/value commitment vs UX actor discovery:** Product legitimately selects beneficiaries/promises; UX investigates real human situations and may challenge narrow assumptions. Do not turn an assumed motivation into approved Product/UX truth.
2. **UX information priority/interaction vs UI visual hierarchy/copy:** UX owns actor-facing information/interaction meaning and timing; UI owns expressive presentation. Copy that changes promises goes to Product; UI findings that change interaction go to UX.
3. **Product domain state vs UX visible state vs Engineering internal state:** Domain truth/effects, actor interaction feedback and technical mechanisms have different owners and must agree; they are not three copies of one authority.
4. **Rancang I01 = \`Product Contract\`:** Consumer meaning is accepted consequential Product information with exact source/approval, but whether a named document is required remains a **R2/R3 compatibility question**. Do not modify D1-approved bytes as an R1 shortcut.
5. **UX output identifier collision:** Existing A2 \`O2 = UX Flow\`, \`O6 = Lo-Fi\`, \`O7 = Interaction & UI State\` are consumed by UI/Rancang; experimental UX v1.1 \`O2 = Experience Concept\`, \`O6 = Scoped Decisions/Handoff\` differs. Meaning-based inspection is required; no automatic renaming or accepted conversion.
6. **Decision-specific maturity:** Engineering can investigate feasibility without complete UI/Lo-Fi where independent of those decisions; binding technical/interaction decisions cannot use provisional policy or invented security protection.
