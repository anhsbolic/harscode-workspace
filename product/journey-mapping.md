# Product Journey Mapping

> Scope: Harscode Product guidance in the current branch. Product truth is owned by the target project; no change to operational main.

Phase: `PROD-JRN`. Output kind: `PROD-JOURNEYS` — Interconnected Product Journeys.

## Purpose

Connect actor experiences to the value promises defined by Product Foundation. Show meaningful before-to-after states and their interconnections. A journey map is not a feature backlog, release order, or workflow engine.

## Method

Start with approved Product Intent, actor values and responsibilities. Describe representative situations: who acts, what they seek, what they can truly know and conclude, what changes, what may fail or remain unknown, and who else is affected.

For each connection, identify the specific required state and distinguish prerequisite, conditional, downstream and feedback relationships. Do not infer a universal gate or a development sequence.

Separate platform facts, actor assertions, independent review, unknowns, hypotheses and approved decisions. Challenge missing value, hidden authority or payment effects, invented prerequisites and unresolved responsibility. Record material Product questions with their owners.

Use an overview plus bounded detail only where useful. No persona matrix or full journey coverage is mandatory.

## Output

Produce a project-owned, revisioned interconnected journey map with actor journeys, meaningful dependencies, status of claims and unresolved decisions. Project-local node IDs may support tracking without becoming Human-facing phase names.

When mapping reveals a material gap in the Foundation value promise, return the gap to the Product owner rather than silently revising it. An approved map does not approve a Product outcome.
