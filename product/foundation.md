# Product Foundation — Value, Authority and Sufficiency

> **Scope:** Phase `PROD-FND`; primary output kind `PROD-INTENT` (Product Intent & Value Definition). Harscode guidance on this branch; Product Truth belongs to the target project, not this file.
> **Owner:** Harscode Product guidance. Material Product Authority and approval belong to the target project.

## Question

**What real human problem is this product trying to address, and what must be true for its promise to be honest?**

Start here when the current Product Authority is absent, contradictory, poorly scoped, or insufficient for choosing outcomes. If a target project already has sufficient approved authority for the active question, use it; do not reopen settled decisions to complete a framework.

## Minimum product understanding

Seek enough reliable context to state:

1. **Purpose and value:** What need or problem exists, for whom, and what change should the product enable? Distinguish hoped-for value from observed evidence.
2. **Actors and responsibilities:** Who initiates, benefits, decides, is affected, supplies information, and has authority? An actor is a role in a product situation; fictional demographic persona profiles are optional and must not be presented as research findings.
3. **Relevant situations:** What causes people to turn to the product, what is difficult or uncertain, and what outcome would make a real difference?
4. **Truth boundaries:** What does the product know, what is merely supplied or asserted by an actor, what is reviewed, inferred, pending, or unknown? What may it **not** claim?
5. **Consequential constraints:** What role, consent, eligibility, safety, privacy, financial or irreversible effects materially constrain the experience? Name the owner of each known constraint.
6. **Connected outcomes and dependencies:** What other actor experiences or product states matter before or after this outcome? Connections are not automatically a release order.
7. **Open decisions and evidence gaps:** Which uncertainties would change product meaning, which can safely remain design/engineering freedom, and which are outside current scope?

This is a **quality test**, not seven mandatory files or a comprehensive whole-product specification.

## Product Value Definition — owned by Foundation

Foundation owns **what value the product promises, for whom, why it matters, which actors contribute or receive value, and what the product may truthfully claim**. Product Design is a consumer of these approved value principles: it determines how users experience, understand and interact with them, but does not independently create consequential business promises.

Capture the smallest sufficient project-owned Value Definition, which may be **a section of Product Intent** rather than a separate artifact:

- **Human and actor value:** actor, initial need/situation, meaningful intended benefit, and evidence standing; distinguish user observation from hypothesized value.
- **Business and product value:** why the product exists, how value, responsibility and evidence flow among actors, and what makes the product viable without inventing targets.
- **Value exchange and responsibilities:** who supplies information, makes decisions, receives value, bears consequences and provides independent review.
- **Product principles and truth boundaries:** approved promises, values the interface must preserve, and unearned claims it must never imply.

Do not specify a complete journey network, all exceptional states, UI hierarchy or implementation here. **Journey Mapping** elaborates the progression and may uncover new value hypotheses. **Outcome Definition** binds scoped value into testable behavior and acceptance. **Product Design** translates approved meaning into interaction, content and visual expression. Design evidence can challenge a Foundation assumption, but any material Product-value change returns to the authorized Product owner.

A Product Intent already sufficient for the next Product decision need not be rewritten simply to populate a template.

## Evidence and authority

| Item | Correct standing |
| --- | --- |
| User observation, interview, usage trace, support report | Evidence; quality and representativeness must be assessed |
| Hypothesis or proposed product promise | Working claim; test or Human decision pending |
| Human-approved product intent/invariant | Binding only in its explicit project/scope/revision |
| Human-approved commitment-specific behavior/requirements | Binding for that commitment when ownership, approval and precedence are explicit; not automatically whole-product policy |
| Prototype, generated text, legacy code, prior discussion, simulated output | Evidence or exploration unless separately approved as a source of authority |
| Unknowns, deliberately parked work | Visible uncertainty; not permission to invent semantics |

Never promote an attractive narrative into a verified user need. When outside research is absent, label actor motivation and demand assumptions honestly. Product judgment may still proceed under acknowledged uncertainty when the risk is bounded and reversible.

## Forming or revising project Product Authority

- **Verify before replacing:** Locate the latest owning project files, approvals, source revisions, contradictory claims and actual product evidence. Existing code is evidence of behavior, not automatically permission to define product meaning.
- **Frame the strongest material gap:** Challenge whether the stated problem is real, the actor receives value, the claim is testable/truthful, and important affected people or tail risks have been omitted.
- **Resolve proportionately:** For high-consequence claims, require stronger evidence, fallback and containment. For low-risk reversible uncertainties, prefer bounded experiments rather than speculative specification.
- **Keep the authority minimal but sufficient:** Capture the actual purpose, relevant actor/value truths, invariants, accepted scope, explicit owner, evidence references and intentionally open decisions. Exact document format is project-owned.
- **Human acceptance and persistence:** A substantive approval must identify what was accepted and at what scope/revision. Record it in the project-owned artifact; verify the persisted state. Chat approval without persistence is a working decision, not durable canonical truth.
- **Revisit only on new material evidence:** A downstream gap may call for clarification; it is not blanket permission to redesign settled authority.

## Sufficiency check

Product Authority is sufficient **for the next decision** when a fresh reader can answer:

- Whose problem and value are in focus, and which claims are still assumptions?
- What known product truth, external claims, and important unknowns must remain distinct?
- Which actor/outcome dependencies matter and what is outside the current scope?
- Who may approve a material semantic change, and where will the decision be persisted?
- Can the next product decision be made without inventing a material premise?

If yes, proceed to experience discovery or commitment work. Do **not** require all future product questions to be solved.

If no, record the narrow blocker and its owner. Exploration can continue as **hypothesis/evidence**, but a binding product or delivery claim cannot silently proceed.
