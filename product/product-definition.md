# Product Definition — Core Concern

> **Standing:** AGREED WORKING RESEARCH DESIGN (2026-11-04). Not operational Harscode policy, a formed Space, or target-project Product authority.
> **Design reference:** experiments/org-01-reverse-lifecycle/product-definition.md and candidate-reference-model.md. Experiment-led, but still challengeable through evidence.
> **Authority:** Harscode owns reusable guidance. Effective Product meaning belongs to the target project and its legitimately authorized Human.

## 1. Purpose and responsibility

Product Definition establishes **what meaningful change a product should enable, for whom, why it matters, and what must be true for the outcome to be legitimate**.

Product owns Product intent/value, Actor Definition & Goals, meaningful Product User Journey, consequential Domain Roles & Authority Rules, bounded outcomes, required domain information/disclosures, business rules, invariants, Success Criteria, Acceptance Criteria and Product decisions.

It does not own UX flows, webpage paths/navigation, visual expression, backend APIs, data models, technical authorization mechanisms, implementation or runtime verification. A concern is not automatically a Phase, Space, Agent, workflow state or file.

## 2. INPUT — Product information requirements

| No. | Information need | Decision-specific requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Product Direction & Initiative Context | Required |
| 2 | Problem Statements & Signals | Required |
| 3 | Candidate Actor & Context | Required |
| 4 | Domain Context & Terminology | Required |
| 5 | Existing Product Behavior & Boundaries | Required for existing products |
| 6 | Existing Business Rules & Policies | Required when relevant |
| 7 | Stakeholder Goals & Decision Authority | Required |
| 8 | Product Success Signals | Required; may be provisional |
| 9 | User Research & Existing Feedback | Consider if available and relevant |
| 10 | Legal, Privacy & Compliance Constraints | Required when material |
| 11 | Known Business & Operational Constraints | Required when material |
| 12 | Related Product Dependencies | As needed |
| 13 | Market & Competitor References | Optional |

These are **information needs**, not thirteen required upstream documents, steps or producer subjects. Inputs may come from project authority, Human decisions, current domain knowledge, research, observed behavior or other concerns. Preserve provenance and distinguish approved authority, observations, external assertions, explicit hypotheses and unknowns. Existing code is evidence of what happens, not automatic authority for what should happen.

Product Contract is an output, not a prerequisite to beginning Product Definition. Relevant existing contracts can be input authority. Bounded exploration may use labeled hypotheses; consequential binding claims need sufficient legitimate authority and evidence. Missing information blocks only the affected decision.

## 3. PROCESS — Nine iterative activities

| No. | Activity | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Product Intent & Problem Definition | Understand problem, value and purpose |
| 2 | Actor & Context Analysis | Understand actors, goals, needs, situations and affected parties |
| 3 | Domain Role & Authority Definition | Define material domain relationships, eligibility, role acquisition/loss, restrictions and effects |
| 4 | Product User Journey Definition | Define meaningful progression, truth/authority transitions, failure and uncertainty |
| 5 | Product Outcome & Scope Definition | Select bounded outcome, scope, real prerequisites and exclusions |
| 6 | Product Requirements & Business Rules | Define consequential behavior, information obligations and invariants |
| 7 | Success & Acceptance Definition | Define legitimate success and observable positive/negative/unknown checks |
| 8 | Product Review & Refinement | Challenge assumptions, contradictions, authority and downstream sufficiency |
| 9 | Human Product Review & Approval | Record legitimate decisions and exact-revision acceptance when needed |

Activities are iterative and proportional, **not a mandatory nine-step waterfall**. Sufficient existing approved Product meaning need not be rediscovered. Other concerns can raise findings but cannot independently change Product authority.

## 4. OUTPUT — Six logical Product deliverables

These are information outputs, **not six mandatory physical files**.

### O1. Product Intent & Value Definition

Purpose, problem, intended value and why it matters; actor/business responsibilities; legitimate promises, truth boundaries, relevant evidence, material assumptions and unknowns. Quality question: can the intended value be understood without representing hypothetical demand as validated observation?

### O2. Actor Definition & Goals

Actor perspective, goals, context, needs, limitations and relevant responsibilities. **Actor ≠ Domain Role:** Actor describes a perspective/goal in a Product situation. Domain Role is actual domain standing, relationship and authority with meaningful creation, change and loss conditions when material. Domain Role is not necessarily technical RBAC. Quality question: can experience decisions be made without inventing consequential actor needs or authority?

### O3. Product User Journey

Meaningful actor/domain progression: starting situation, goals, prerequisites, material actions, state/authority changes, pre-effect comprehension, legitimate success, denial, failure, partial and uncertain results, inspectability and affected actors when applicable. Product owns **meaningful consequences**, not screen count, UX interaction sequencing, URLs or navigation. Quality question: can experience design reconstruct the bounded before-to-after meaning?

### O4. Selected Product Outcome

Coherent bounded actor-value commitment, selection owner and standing, scope, real prerequisites, dependencies, exclusions and non-guarantees. Selection authorizes Product elaboration only; not contract approval, capability invocation or delivery. Quality question: can it be distinguished from neighboring and unavailable outcomes without false success?

### O5. Product Contract

Self-contained accepted consequential meaning for the bounded outcome:
- Outcome, applicable scope, material non-guarantees and joint Success Criteria.
- Actor/domain role definitions, eligibility and authority/role transitions when consequential.
- **Consequential Content & Information Requirements:** information to collect, communicate, make inspectable or understand; evidence/provenance and verified-vs-asserted/pending distinctions; pre-effect disclosure and prohibited claims.
- Product behavior, business rules, preconditions, invariants, material negative/partial/unknown results.
- Observable Acceptance Criteria traceable to Product obligations and success, including false-success/authority protection.
- Real prerequisites, unavailable dependencies, scope exclusions, delegated solution freedom, unresolved decisions and owners.

Binding Product semantics must be directly understandable in the approved contract, **not hidden in research IDs or conversational history**. Supporting actor/journey material may be referenced for context, but cannot conceal binding rules. Design owns information hierarchy and final UI expression; Engineering owns technical mechanisms.

### O6. Product Review & Approval Record

Legitimate Human decisions, accepted/rejected/deferred scope, exact immutable artifact revisions, evidence, limitations, remaining open issues and effective decision standing. Approval record may be external to the approved Contract so recording approval does not mutate its approved bytes. Quality question: can a fresh consumer verify exactly who accepted what, for which scope and immutable revision?

## 5. OUTCOME — Product quality properties

| Property | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Clear Purpose | Problem, intention and value understood |
| Actor-Centered | Relevant actors, goals, context and meaningful journey understood |
| Defined Success | Legitimate success and material non-success conditions explicit |
| Truthful & Bounded | Consequential rules, authority, exclusions, failure and uncertainty accurate |
| Testable | Material obligations have observable acceptance |
| Authoritative | Legitimate decision ownership, approval standing and revisions durable |

These are **Product Definition** qualities, not proof of user-demand validation, independent usability, implementation, integration or delivered actor benefit. Sufficiency is decision-specific.

## 6. Semantic and authority boundaries

- **Product Definition:** Product value, actor/domain meaning, consequential information, business requirements, bounded outcome and acceptance.
- **Interaction & Experience Design:** interaction flow, IA, navigation/webpage structure and UI-state interaction decisions under accepted Product meaning.
- **Visual Interface & Content Design:** final interface expression, UI copy, responsive visual design, components/assets within binding meanings.
- **Engineering Solution Design:** architecture, technical interfaces, data/state, security/auth mechanisms and technical verification obligations.
- **Engineering Development:** actual authorized implementation and performed evidence.
- **Product Verification & Acceptance:** observed comparison with approved Product claims, without self-generating policy.

These are **functional responsibilities**, not named mandatory stages/Spaces/Agents or exclusive providers. An actual project owns Product decisions and applicable security/privacy protections.

## 7. Information consumption & responsibility interfaces

**What is required precedes who supplies or consumes it.**

| Receiving functional responsibility | Product information needed when material |
| --- | --- |
| Interaction & Experience Design | Product intent, Actor Definition, Product User Journey, selected outcome, information obligations, behavior, acceptance |
| Visual Interface & Content Design | Binding Product meaning, consequential disclosures and truthful result-state distinctions |
| Engineering Solution Design | Approved bounded obligations, Domain Role meanings, invariants, success and Acceptance Criteria |
| Engineering Development | Applicable accepted Product behavior/acceptance, plus separately owned Design, technical, security constraints |
| Product Verification & Acceptance | Approved success/non-success, Product obligations, observed evidence and limits |
| Product Decision & Reconciliation | Material new evidence, affected Product claims, consequences, legitimate owner |

Consumers obtain needed information from **effective original owning sources or legitimate handoff**, not a required preceding Space. Multiple consumers can use one accepted truth without duplicating authority. Relevant inputs are validated as required information → actual source/revision/standing → decision sufficiency. Missing material information holds only affected work. A delegated UX/Engineering choice is not a Product gap.

Concrete Harscode subject/capability mapping belongs to navigation and authorized project assignments, **not this semantic contract**. Replacing a subject does not change an information contract unless required meaning changes.

## 8. Review, approval and completion

Consequential binding decisions require material source reconciliation, legitimate owner approval, exact-revision persistence and appropriate consumer input checks. Exploration can continue under explicit hypotheses where safe. Product approval does not confer Design/Engineering approval, capability formation, invocation, protected writes or runtime claims.

See [Authority & Approval](authority-and-approval.md) for decision standing, and [Consumer Handoff](consumer-handoff.md) for information discovery and compatibility.

## 9. Implementation and documentation freedom

No mandatory Product Space, Agent team, stage order, thirteen files, nine gates, six output files, central registry, universal workflow or technology is implied. Target projects own actual accepted artifact format, language and location.

**Final rule: Product Definition depends on what information and decisions are necessary—not who happens to produce or consume them.**
