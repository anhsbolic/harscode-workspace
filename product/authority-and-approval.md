# Product Authority & Approval

> **Standing:** AGREED WORKING RESEARCH DESIGN (2026-11-04); not operational Harscode policy or target-project authority.
> **Purpose:** Legitimate Product decision ownership, standing, exact-revision approval and supersession.

## 1. Principle

**Product authority comes from legitimate decision ownership and scope—not from the Phase, Space, Agent, tool or person who authored an artifact.** Reusable Harscode guidance does not grant project Product decision power.

## 2. Functional responsibilities

| Responsibility | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Product analysis/recommendation | Inspect, challenge and propose; not automatically approve |
| Delegated Product decision | Within exact delegated authority only |
| Consequential Product decision | By applicable legitimate Human owner |
| Artifact authoring | Represents Product meaning without independently authorizing it |
| Product review | Checks semantics, evidence and sufficiency; not an approval |
| Product approval | Accepts exact content and scope for effective revision |
| Consumer acceptance | Assesses its own actual input; does not overwrite Product truth |

The **concrete** authorized decision owner must be discoverable from target-project authority and assignment, not assumed from a named reusable Harscode subject.

## 3. Standing

Distinguish **observed evidence**, **working hypothesis**, **proposed decision**, **approved decision**, **currently effective authority**, **superseded historical decision** and **unresolved/unknown**. They are semantic distinctions, not mandatory runtime states.

Artifact existence, historical use, newer timestamps or Human approval of another revision do not confer current authority. Product approval does not empirically validate user demand.

## 4. Establish effective authority

Locate actual owning project artifacts, exact revisions, decision owners, approval records and precedence. Keep observations, actor/external assertions, hypotheses and historical examples separate from binding meaning. Existing code is evidence, not automatic Product policy.

When two authorities materially conflict, name both exact sources, consequences and owner. Do not silently choose a preferred source. Applicable legal, privacy, security and protected-action limits remain binding.

## 5. Decision sufficiency and Product review

Challenge problem/value evidence, actor goals and meaningful journey, consequential Domain Role and information obligations, business rules, SC/REQ/AC coherence, negative/partial/uncertain paths, dependencies, affected parties and consumer completeness.

Scale scrutiny with consequence, irreversibility, privacy/security implications and consumer blast radius. Not every unresolved question blocks; only the affected binding claim does where material. Bounded exploratory hypotheses remain non-binding.

## 6. Human Product decision

Present a precise question and proposed semantic change, affected project/outcome/scope, existing effective sources/revisions, evidence vs assumptions, risk/alternatives where material, recommendation, legitimate Human owner, and exact candidate artifact/revision.

No internal review PASS, conversation acknowledgment or template completion automatically approves unmentioned Product semantics.

## 7. Exact-revision approval and persistence

**Product Contract states binding meaning; Product Review & Approval Record identifies exact accepted bytes, who accepted them, affected scope, decision evidence and limitations.**

Minimum durable record: project/outcome identity, legitimate approver, decision, scope and exclusions, exact Contract path and blob/commit revision, decision reference/time, material unknowns and any supersession relation.

**Do not edit the approved Contract merely to embed its approval label or own SHA, then treat the old approval as covering new bytes.** Keep the Approval Record separate when needed. If acceptance happens before final artifact revision is known, preserve immutable reviewed content and reconcile the persisted bytes; materially changed content requires new review. Unpersisted chat approval remains a working decision, not durable project authority.

## 8. Revision and supersession

Material changed meaning requires new legitimate owner decision and exact-revision approval. A revision called editorial is not automatically semantic-equivalent. An authorized identifier/path migration may preserve approval meaning only with old approved bytes, complete old→new mapping, verified semantic equivalence and newly effective reference. Never retroactively rewrite approval history.

New evidence can justify reopening an accepted decision but does not itself override effective sources. Revalidate affected consumer edges proportionally, without gratuitous reapproval of unrelated choices.

## 9. Boundaries

Product approval is **not** Design acceptance, Engineering solution approval, reusable capability formation, invocation, protected-write permission, canonical API adoption, runtime verification, deployment or proof of delivered user value.

Discovering a Product gap does not grant the discoverer authority to amend it. Return the exact affected decision and evidence to its authorized owner.

## 10. Completion

Authority is sufficient for an actual decision when a fresh reader can reconstruct accepted meaning, legitimate Human decision, scope, exact effective revision and material limitations without conversational memory. No mandatory global registry, workflow or approval ceremony is created.

**Final rule: authority follows accepted meaning, legitimate owner, scope and revision—not the authoring subject.**
