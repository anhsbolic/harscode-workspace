# Product Consumer Handoff

> **Standing:** AGREED WORKING RESEARCH DESIGN (2026-11-04). Not operational Harscode policy, project Product authority or execution authorization.
> **Purpose:** Information-contract-based Product consumption without dependencies on named phases, Spaces, Agents, teams or technology.

## 1. Principle

**Product handoff exposes the accepted information a receiving responsibility needs; it does not prescribe the subject that performs that responsibility.** Direct approved project sources or a concise discoverable handoff pointer are valid. Never create duplicate Product authority merely to fill a document slot.

## 2. What, responsibility, who

| Layer | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Information contract | What exact accepted information, evidence, constraints and standing are needed? |
| Functional responsibility | What decision or kind of work needs it? |
| Concrete subject | Which actual Human, team, Agent or capability is authorized now? |

Only the first two define reusable Product consumption. The third is resolved through actual project assignment and applicable capability authority. One subject can cover multiple bounded responsibilities; one responsibility can have multiple possible subjects.

## 3. Product information types

Product Definition exposes six logical outputs: **Product Intent & Value Definition**, **Actor Definition & Goals**, **Product User Journey**, **Selected Product Outcome**, **Product Contract**, and **Product Review & Approval Record**.

They are not six mandatory files or one mandatory package. The approved Product Contract must directly expose its own binding Product rules; supporting discovery material must not hide additional obligations.

## 4. Receiving responsibilities

| Responsibility | Product information needed when material |
| --- | --- |
| Interaction & Experience Design | Intent, actors/goals, Product journey, bounded outcome, consequential information and behavior, acceptance constraints |
| Visual Interface & Content Design | Binding Product meaning, required disclosures/provenance and truthful result states |
| Engineering Solution Design | Accepted bounded Product behavior, domain roles/authority, invariants, success and acceptance |
| Engineering Development | Applicable Product behavior/acceptance **plus separately owned** Design, technical, security and implementation inputs |
| Product Verification & Acceptance | Accepted outcome, SC/REQ/AC, actual observations and evidence limitations |
| Product Decision & Reconciliation | Material findings, affected accepted claims, evidence, consequences and legitimate owner |

These are **functional categories**, not a chain of named Harscode Spaces. Product is not the sole source of Engineering or Design inputs.

## 5. Minimum consumable handoff

For the actual intended decision, answer: **objective**, **binding actor/domain meaning**, **authority standing**, **exact effective sources/revisions**, **observable acceptance**, **real prerequisites and known availability**, **delegated freedoms**, **unknowns/exclusions/blocked edges**, and **legitimate owner for consequential gaps**.

These are logical fields. A link index is sufficient if current, durable and navigable. Do not reproduce binding SC/REQ/AC in a second master handoff.

## 6. Decision-specific sufficiency

Consumer readiness means this receiving responsibility can make its permitted decision without inventing material Product meaning or bypassing effective constraints.

Exploration may use explicit proposed Product hypotheses. Consequential implementation/design decisions require actual accepted Product obligations where material. Distinguish missing Product meaning, delegated solution question, missing Design/technical source, unverified runtime fact and missing permission. Hold only the affected edge.

## 7. Original source fidelity

**A self-contained Product Contract does not waive a receiver's required original-source inspection or other applicable authority checks.**

Binding Product semantics must be directly understandable without reconstructing upstream research IDs or chat history. Receivers must still satisfy their own original-source, exact Product approval, Design/technical/security/project-standard and authorization obligations.

Research links provide provenance, not hidden additional binding Product rules. Material contradictions go to the legitimate Product owner; a receiver must not invent a replacement rule.

## 8. Two compatibility checks

**Generic compatibility:** If an applicable reusable receiving Input Contract exists, check information type, semantic completeness, required standing and actual revisions. If none applies, mark **NOT APPLICABLE**, not a fictional Space contract.

**Project-bound compatibility:** For each actual intended assignment, inspect whether exact effective Product sources meet its project-specific objective, scope, authority, accepted standards, security and source constraints. This check is needed even if generic compatibility is not applicable.

Generic fit is not actual project fit. Unknown future consumers are **NOT CHECKED**; relevant gaps are **CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED / UNVERIFIED** with exact missing evidence. Do not fabricate a consumer acceptance test.

## 9. Feedback and reconciliation

New consequential Product contradictions, missing role authority, information/disclosure gaps or conflicting evidence return to the actual Product decision owner with exact source, scope, consequence and required decision. Feedback is evidence, not auto-amendment. Independent safe, authorized work may continue.

No handoff automatically dispatches a Space or authorizes another concern's writes.

## 10. Separate acceptance and outcome claims

Product handoff does not approve downstream Design/Engineering choices, form or invoke capabilities, grant protected writes, adopt canonical technical interfaces, prove integrated runtime or deliver actor value. Internal source review is not independent fresh-consumer validation; mocks/prototypes do not prove real system behavior.

## 11. Completion

For the stated receiving responsibility, handoff is sufficient when effective accepted Product information, revisions, decision boundaries and unresolved material issues are discoverable and understandable without conversational recall. No fixed document packet, global dependency registry, mandatory stage sequence or subject binding is implied.

**Final rule: information flows according to semantic need and legitimate responsibility—not named producers and consumers.**
